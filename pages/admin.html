Destination coverage per division

Content checklist

  • Coordinates within Bangladesh bounds (20.5–26.7 N, 88.0–92.7 E)
  • Best-time window and duration filled for every record
  • Price floor reviewed each season (Oct & Apr)
  • Long description 200–500 words, no supplier marketing copy
  • Bangla name present for records shown on the Bangla site
  • Photo licence recorded in ATTRIBUTION.md
