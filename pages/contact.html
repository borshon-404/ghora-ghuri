Where we are

Daulatpur sits on the Khulna–Mongla highway, 12 km from the Sundarban boat launches at Noapara and Dhubahar, and 45 minutes from Mongla port.

Reach us by road

  • From Dhaka - N810 via Bhanga bridge, 330 km, 6–7 h by car; shuttle buses from Gabtoli to Khulna point
  • From Bagerhat - R760, 38 km, 45 min
  • From Mongla - D704, 32 km, 40 min
  • By train - Chhayanat Mandap / Sundarban Express to Khulna railway station, then a 20-minute CNG
