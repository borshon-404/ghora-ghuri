The Sundarbans, handled properly

Forest permits, licensed vessels and a fixed route - or it does not happen

Every Sundarban itinerary GHORA GHURI sells runs on a forest-department approved route (Katka, Haringata, Dublar Char or the Mongla side) with a licensed vessel owner, an on-board guide and the permit paperwork filed before departure. Deer at sunset on the char, kingfishers at first light, and a real chance of a tiger track - never a promise of one.

  • Cabin cruiser or traditional mining boat, both with attached bathrooms
  • Two, three and five-night route options; full-day Khulna-forest run for time-pressed guests
  • Hilsa and rice cooked on board; vegetarian and medical diets on request
  • Monsoon policy: boats do not enter the reserve in rough-weather windows, deposits roll forward
